Rest Alert — Privacy Policy

Effective June 19, 2026 · Last updated November 12, 2026

Rest Alert (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a personal wellness app that reads your heart-rate and heart-rate-variability (HRV) data from your smartwatch and sends you rest reminders. This policy explains exactly what data we handle, why, and your choices. We collect the minimum needed to make the app work, and we never sell your data.

Developer: AJ Miller T. Perez · No verified public support email is currently published. For account deletion, use the linked Telegram bot’s /delete command.

1. Data we collect

Rest Alert reads the following from Health Connect, only after you grant permission:

DataSourceWhy we need it
Heart rate (bpm)Your smartwatch via Health Connect Detect sustained high heart rate while at rest
Heart-rate variability (HRV / RMSSD, ms)Your smartwatch via Health Connect Detect low HRV (fatigue / under-recovery)
Exercise / workout sessionsHealth Connect Suppress alerts during workouts (high HR is expected then)

We also store your Telegram chat ID (to deliver alerts) and your app settings (thresholds, quiet hours, pause state). We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

2. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to decide whether to send a rest alert, deliver those alerts and a daily morning recovery summary to your Telegram chat, and show your recent readings in the app. No advertising, profiling, or sale of data.

3. Where your data goes

The mobile app sends readings to the backend address configured by its operator. The current backend source stores readings in a SQLite database; the hosting provider and live deployment protections have not been independently verified. Production deployments should use HTTPS. Alert messages and summaries may be delivered through the Telegram Bot API, subject to Telegram’s Privacy Policy. Health Connect and any configured hosting service have their own privacy practices.

4. Health Connect

Our use of Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy. The app requests read-only access to Heart Rate, HRV, and Exercise for the functions described above. Readings are sent to the configured backend, while Telegram may receive alert text and summaries derived from those readings. You can revoke Health Connect access in Android settings.

5. Data retention & deletion

The current backend source does not implement an automatic 30-day purge. Stored readings, alerts, settings and summaries remain until deleted. To delete the linked account and its records from the app database, send /delete to your linked Telegram bot. You can also stop collecting new readings by pausing tracking or revoking Health Connect access. Deletion from the app database does not automatically remove previously delivered Telegram messages or records retained separately by infrastructure providers.

6. Security

The backend source uses a shared API key to restrict requests. This is not individual account-level authorization. HTTPS/TLS and an appropriately configured production key are necessary for secure operation; the live deployment has not been independently validated. Do not share sensitive health readings with an unverified backend.

7. Children

Rest Alert is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your rights

You may have rights to access, correct or delete your data under applicable law. The linked Telegram bot supports account deletion through /delete. A verified private support channel for other privacy requests has not yet been published; do not post your health readings in public issue trackers.

9. Not a medical device

Rest Alert is a general wellness tool. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a qualified health professional for medical advice.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

11. Contact

AJ Miller T. Perez — the Rest Alert project repository accepts non-sensitive policy feedback. Do not share personal health information in public discussions. A private support contact is not yet verified.
