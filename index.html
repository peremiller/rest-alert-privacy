Rest Alert — Privacy Policy

Effective June 19, 2026 · Last updated June 19, 2026

Rest Alert (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a personal wellness app that reads your heart-rate and heart-rate-variability (HRV) data from your smartwatch and sends you rest reminders. This policy explains exactly what data we handle, why, and your choices. We collect the minimum needed to make the app work, and we never sell your data.

Developer: AJ Miller T. Perez · Contact: [your-support-email]

1. Data we collect

Rest Alert reads the following from Health Connect, only after you grant permission:

DataSourceWhy we need it
Heart rate (bpm)Your smartwatch via Health Connect Detect sustained high heart rate while at rest
Heart-rate variability (HRV / RMSSD, ms)Your smartwatch via Health Connect Detect low HRV (fatigue / under-recovery)
Exercise / workout sessionsHealth Connect Suppress alerts during workouts (high HR is expected then)

We also store your Telegram chat ID (to deliver alerts) and your app settings (thresholds, quiet hours, pause state). We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

2. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to decide whether to send a rest alert, deliver those alerts and a daily morning recovery summary to your Telegram chat, and show your recent readings in the app. No advertising, profiling, or sale of data.

3. Where your data goes

Health readings are sent over HTTPS to the app’s backend on Fly.io and stored in a private database. Alerts are delivered via the Telegram Bot API (subject to Telegram’s Privacy Policy). These are the only third parties involved.

4. Health Connect

Our use of Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy. We request read-only access to Heart Rate, HRV, and Exercise, use it only for the features above, and do not share it with third parties except Telegram (to deliver your messages). You can revoke access anytime in the Health Connect app.

5. Data retention & deletion

Readings are auto-purged after [30] days. You can stop tracking anytime in the app. To erase everything immediately, send /delete to the Telegram bot or email [your-support-email].

6. Security

Data in transit is protected with HTTPS/TLS; the backend is access-controlled with an API key. No method is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

7. Children

Rest Alert is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your rights

You may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact [your-support-email] to exercise these rights.

9. Not a medical device

Rest Alert is a general wellness tool. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a qualified health professional for medical advice.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
